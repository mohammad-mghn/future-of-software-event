# v2 slide review (2026-11-26)

The host's verdict on every v2 slide, collected question by question. This is the input for v3.

**Legend:** Keep = keep as is · Deeper = keep the idea, go deeper · Cut = drop · Redo = rewrite from scratch

## What the comments have in common

1. **The host has to understand every sentence.** Several slides were unclear even to the host (24, 26, 39, 40, 45, 46, 48). If the host can't explain a slide, it's wrong.
2. **Data that doesn't explain anything gets cut**: a tiny Iranian sample, "AI's share is 10–51%", a history chart, "9 in 10 use agents".
3. **Titles and labels without analysis are too shallow.** Every list item needs a why and a how (34, 36, 37, 42).
4. **Weak conclusions** (15, 17, 44, 53). A conclusion has to be earned by the analysis before it.
5. **Now and where it's heading.** Not forecasts, but each finding should carry its implication forward ("it didn't crash, but will it?").
6. **Persian wording**: «فرم» means nothing here (23), and slide 24 doesn't make sense in Persian.
